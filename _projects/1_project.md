This project develops a discrete-time manufacturing simulation to support equipment investment decisions at SK Hynix under demand uncertainty.

Using event logs from production systems, I modeled process flows and bottlenecks across five major semiconductor process-equipment segments. The simulation evaluates capacity risks under multiple sales-plan scenarios and derives resilient investment strategies amid demand volatility.

**Methods:** Discrete-event simulation, scenario analysis, capacity planning

**Advisor:** Professor Sang-won Kim, KAIST College of Business
