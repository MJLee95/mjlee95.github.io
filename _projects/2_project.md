This project develops a scenario-driven demand forecasting and capacity planning framework for a pharmaceutical firm facing multi-year demand uncertainty.

Using Monte Carlo simulation, I built forecasting models covering a 4–5 year planning horizon and assessed 3-stage production capacity under shifting product mix. The project also mapped cross-functional decision flows to identify coordination bottlenecks affecting planning and investment decisions.

**Methods:** Monte Carlo simulation, capacity planning, scenario analysis

**Advisor:** Professor Bo-won Kim, KAIST College of Business
